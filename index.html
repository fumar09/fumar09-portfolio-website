Process

How I approach design and presentation work.

I want my portfolio to feel like a professional designer's portfolio, so I organize my work around a process that keeps the result clean, consistent, and intentional.

01

Understand the goal

I start by identifying what needs to feel clearer, stronger, more premium, or easier to use.

02

Build the hierarchy

I organize layout, spacing, and content flow so the screen guides attention properly.

03

Refine the visual system

I shape typography, color, and components so the page feels consistent and professional.

04

Polish for real screens

I check responsiveness and presentation details so the design still feels strong in the browser.
